The Innovation Office (IO) of Instituto Curvelo is responsible for coordinating
technological innovation and technology transfer activities. Its main activities include:

- Safeguarding the Innovation Policy of Instituto Curvelo
- Managing intellectual property assets
- Identifying and creating opportunities
- Leveraging resources from national public policies
- Managing and promoting partnerships

The IO is led by the Innovation Director, {{% mention "flavio" %}}.
